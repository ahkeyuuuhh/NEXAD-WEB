NEXAD User Manual

Learn how to use NEXAD's consultation and classroom features

1

Request a Consultation

Navigate to your classroom and tap the "Request Consultation" button. Select your preferred date and time, choose a category (Academic, Personal, Career), and describe your concern.

2

Wait for Approval

Your teacher will review your request and either approve or suggest an alternative time. You'll receive a notification once your consultation is confirmed.

3

Attend the Consultation

At the scheduled time, go to "My Consultations" and join the session. Discuss your concerns with your teacher in a private, focused environment.

4

Review and Follow Up

After the consultation, review any notes or action items. You can request follow-up consultations if needed.

1

Join a Classroom

Enter the classroom code provided by your teacher. Tap "Join Classroom" on your dashboard and input the code to gain access.

2

View Announcements

Check the classroom feed for important announcements, assignments, and updates from your teacher. Enable notifications to stay informed.

3

Submit Assignments

When assignments are posted, tap to view details, upload your work, and submit before the deadline. Track your submission status in real-time.

4

Communicate with Teacher

Use the messaging feature to ask questions, request clarifications, or discuss classroom matters with your teacher privately.

1

Review Consultation Requests

Access your consultation inbox to view pending requests from students. Review the category, reason, and preferred time for each request.

2

Approve or Reschedule

Approve requests that fit your schedule or suggest alternative times. Students will be notified of your decision immediately.

3

Conduct the Session

At the scheduled time, access the consultation from your dashboard. Take notes during the session to track student progress and concerns.

4

Document and Follow Up

After the consultation, add notes and action items. Schedule follow-up sessions if needed and track student progress over time.

1

Create a Classroom

Set up a new classroom by providing a name, description, and subject. A unique classroom code will be generated for students to join.

2

Post Announcements

Share important updates, reminders, and information with your class. Announcements appear in the classroom feed and trigger notifications.

3

Create and Manage Assignments

Post assignments with clear instructions, due dates, and file attachments. Review student submissions and provide feedback directly in the app.

4

Monitor Student Progress

Track student engagement, assignment completion rates, and consultation history. Use insights to provide targeted support where needed.
