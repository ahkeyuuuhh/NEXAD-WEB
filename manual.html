NEXAD User Manual

Learn how to use NEXAD's consultation and classroom features

1

Request a Consultation

Navigate to your classroom and tap the "Request Consultation" button. Select your preferred date and time, choose a category (Academic, Personal, Career), and describe your concern.

2

Wait for Approval

Your teacher will review your request and either approve or suggest an alternative time. You'll receive a notification once your consultation is confirmed.

3

Attend the Consultation

At the scheduled time, go to "My Consultations" and join the session. Discuss your concerns with your teacher in a private, focused environment.

4

Review and Follow Up

After the consultation, review any notes or action items. You can request follow-up consultations if needed.

1

Download Jitsi Meet (Required)

Important: For the best virtual consultation experience, download the Jitsi Meet app before your session:

  • iOS: Download from the App Store
  • Android: Download from Google Play

Jitsi Meet provides better video quality, audio clarity, and connection stability compared to browser-based meetings.

2

Request Virtual Consultation

When requesting a consultation, select "Virtual" as the consultation type. Choose your preferred date, time, and describe the topic you'd like to discuss.

3

Prepare for Your Session

Before the meeting:

  • Test your camera and microphone
  • Find a quiet, well-lit location
  • Prepare any questions or materials
  • Ensure stable internet connection
4

Join the Virtual Meeting

At the scheduled time, go to "My Consultations" and tap "Join Virtual Meeting". The Jitsi Meet app will open automatically with your consultation room. Your teacher will join shortly.

5

During the Consultation

Use Jitsi Meet's features:

  • Toggle camera/microphone as needed
  • Use chat for sharing links or notes
  • Share your screen if discussing documents
  • Raise hand feature for questions
6

After the Session

Once the consultation ends, you can review any notes or action items in the app. Schedule follow-up sessions if needed.

1

Join a Classroom

Enter the classroom code provided by your teacher. Tap "Join Classroom" on your dashboard and input the code to gain access.

2

View Announcements

Check the classroom feed for important announcements, assignments, and updates from your teacher. Enable notifications to stay informed.

3

Submit Assignments

When assignments are posted, tap to view details, upload your work, and submit before the deadline. Track your submission status in real-time.

4

Communicate with Teacher

Use the messaging feature to ask questions, request clarifications, or discuss classroom matters with your teacher privately.

1

Review Consultation Requests

Access your consultation inbox to view pending requests from students. Review the category, reason, and preferred time for each request.

2

Approve or Reschedule

Approve requests that fit your schedule or suggest alternative times. Students will be notified of your decision immediately.

3

Conduct the Session

At the scheduled time, access the consultation from your dashboard. Take notes during the session to track student progress and concerns.

4

Document and Follow Up

After the consultation, add notes and action items. Schedule follow-up sessions if needed and track student progress over time.

1

Download Jitsi Meet (Recommended)

Important: For optimal virtual consultation quality, we recommend downloading the Jitsi Meet app:

  • iOS: Download from the App Store
  • Android: Download from Google Play

Jitsi Meet offers superior video/audio quality and reliability. Inform your students to download it as well for the best experience.

2

Review Virtual Consultation Requests

Check your consultation inbox for virtual meeting requests. Review the student's topic, preferred time, and any special requirements they've mentioned.

3

Approve and Schedule

Approve the virtual consultation request or suggest an alternative time that works better. The system will automatically create a unique Jitsi meeting room for the session.

4

Prepare Your Environment

Before the meeting:

  • Test your camera, microphone, and speakers
  • Choose a quiet, professional background
  • Review student's consultation topic
  • Prepare any materials or resources
  • Ensure stable internet connection
5

Start the Virtual Meeting

At the scheduled time, tap "Start Virtual Meeting" from your consultations list. The Jitsi Meet app will open with your private consultation room. Wait for the student to join.

6

Conduct the Session

Use Jitsi Meet's professional features:

  • Screen sharing: Share presentations or documents
  • Chat: Send links, notes, or resources
  • Recording: Record sessions (with student consent)
  • Mute controls: Manage audio as needed
  • Virtual backgrounds: Maintain professionalism
7

Document and Follow Up

After the consultation, add detailed notes in the app about what was discussed, action items, and recommendations. Schedule follow-up sessions if needed and track student progress.

8

Best Practices

  • Start meetings on time to respect student schedules
  • Keep sessions focused on the consultation topic
  • Encourage students to use video for better engagement
  • Take notes during the session for accurate records
  • End with clear action items and next steps
  • Remind students about Jitsi Meet for future sessions
1

Create a Classroom

Set up a new classroom by providing a name, description, and subject. A unique classroom code will be generated for students to join.

2

Post Announcements

Share important updates, reminders, and information with your class. Announcements appear in the classroom feed and trigger notifications.

3

Create and Manage Assignments

Post assignments with clear instructions, due dates, and file attachments. Review student submissions and provide feedback directly in the app.

4

Monitor Student Progress

Track student engagement, assignment completion rates, and consultation history. Use insights to provide targeted support where needed.
